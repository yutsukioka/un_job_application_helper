# World Bank proficiency: source context

Research baseline: 11 September 2026. The applicant confirmed that the
World Bank form offered **High, Medium and Low**. This is observed form
information, not a public definition of the choices. Reuse supplied current
form instructions if they differ.

No behavioral definitions, ATS weight or automatic rejection threshold for
these choices were established in the public WBG sources checked. Do not
present the skill's advisory rubric as a WBG rule or infer the Bank's
configuration from generic Cornerstone features. Broad WBG-funded digital
skills frameworks concern other populations and are not recruitment scales.

## Useful sources

- [WBG Core Competencies](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/521791568041235683-0220012019/original/WBGCoreCompetenciesFinal.pdf):
  distinguishes core behaviors, technical competencies and managerial
  competencies, with grade descriptions. Useful for matching evidence to
  role scope; no grade-to-High/Medium/Low conversion is established.
- [WBG Hiring Process](https://www.worldbank.org/ext/en/careers/hiring-process)
  and [Careers FAQ](https://www.worldbank.org/ext/en/careers/faqs): explain
  screening, review and application preparation. They do not publish a
  proficiency-field scoring formula.
- [Portal Help/Contact FAQ](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/06bfd15cd64d62bd3225aa7d7c755ffa-0220012025/original/Help-Contact-FAQs.pdf):
  directly linked from the recruitment portal during research; application
  help and support contact, without a definition of the three choices.
- [OPM proficiency guidance](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/career-paths-for-federal-program-and-project-management-guide/appendix-e/):
  external inspiration for considering task difficulty and guidance needed.
  It is not WBG policy and no numerical crosswalk is established.
- [Council of Europe language self-assessment grid](https://www.coe.int/en/web/portfolio/self-assessment-grid):
  useful descriptors for language activities. No official CEFR-to-WBG
  dropdown conversion is established.

These links supply context; the vacancy, applicant evidence and actual
field instructions govern the generated entries. Routine generation does
not require repeating the hiring-process research. Check current primary
sources when asked to verify a policy or when a field's behavior changes.

## Decision examples

- A candidate personally built and validated complex reporting systems
  across several projects and coached analysts: High in an accurately
  scoped analytical skill may be justified. Merely commissioning those
  systems does not establish hands-on software proficiency.
- A CV documents a language used for independently writing reports,
  chairing meetings and negotiating substantive issues: this supports a
  stronger recommendation than a duty station where the language is common.
- A relevant certification is earned but its forced proficiency field has
  no definition: preserve the credential as verified evidence and hold only
  its rating. Generate independently evidenced skill/language rows.
- Option 6 lists a skill as central to a ten-year role: centrality and role
  duration alone still do not establish High proficiency or ten years of
  continuous practice.
