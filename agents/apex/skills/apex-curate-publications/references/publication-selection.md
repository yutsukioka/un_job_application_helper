# Publication selection and attribution

Use these recommendations for placement, not as an exhaustive UNESCO eligibility
policy. Actual vacancy/field instructions govern. Factual support, destination
suitability and relevance are separate assessments; type alone does not decide.

| Work | Usually useful when | Checks and likely alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Published journal article, book or chapter | Credited applicant and useful subject matter. | Preserve author order/title and actual review status. An academic-looking outlet does not establish peer review. Co-authorship is valid. |
| Public institutional report, policy brief, evaluation, technical manual or guideline | Substantive supported authorship or another accurately representable credited role. | Corporate authorship and personal contribution are separate facts. Producing, supervising or funding is not automatically authorship; use employment achievements if the row would conceal the distinction. |
| Thesis/dissertation | Relevant research evidence in a destination accepting that output. | A deposited thesis is not automatically a peer-reviewed article. Preserve its type; consider an education/research placement if the controls cannot represent it truthfully. |
| Published conference paper/proceeding | A substantive paper exists with attributable credits. | Distinguish a full paper, published abstract, poster and oral presentation. Attendance alone belongs elsewhere. |
| Public preprint/working paper | Stable public record and accurate representation of type/status. | Do not call it an accepted/published journal article. A released working-paper series can be a publication in its own right; preserve actual review status. |
| Accepted/in-press work | Supported acceptance and destination allowing an explicit status. | Acceptance date is not publication year. Without a permitted status distinction, hold/use elsewhere; blank Year does not solve this. |
| Submitted manuscript, draft or internal/unpublished output | Destination explicitly accepts works in progress or samples. | Otherwise use appropriately described work/research experience. Never publicize confidential work to manufacture a publication entry. |
| Signed substantive professional blog, commentary, article or op-ed | Publicly issued, attributable, relevant and suitable for the requested scope. | Preserve actual outlet/type; do not infer research or peer review. Routine personal updates/promotional snippets add little. No journal-only default. |
| Edited/translated work | Credited role can be preserved in the destination. | Editing/translation differ from authorship. A labelled selected-works list or achievement can avoid a misleading bare row. |
| Acknowledgement, reviewer, interview subject, foreword or coordination credit | Relevant contribution can be described precisely. | Acknowledgement/review is not authorship. A separately authored foreword may be distinct if supported; otherwise prefer contribution/experience placement. |
| Dataset, software, multimedia or exhibition | Destination accepts the actual output type. | A DOI does not make it an article. Prefer portfolio, research-output or achievement placement if publication controls cannot represent it. |

Do not require a DOI, English text, recent date or fixed count. An older directly
relevant work can be stronger evidence than a recent unrelated one. Honor a
request for all eligible works; explain selective exclusions.

## Representation examples

- A supplied bibliography explicitly credits the applicant as co-author of a
  published report. Exact title and supported subject fit UNESCO controls.
  Unknown year choices leave Year blank without blocking INCLUDE; full credits
  and the known year remain in the ledger.
- A report acknowledges workshop coordination. This can substantiate work
  experience, but a bare publication row would obscure the role. Recommend an
  employment-achievement placement without inventing authorship or editing the
  existing EHF.
- An acceptance letter establishes acceptance, not publication. If the controls
  cannot display the material status, hold the item. A blank Year is insufficient.
- Prefer one final article over its preprint unless the user requests a version
  inventory; retain their relationship without doubling the publication count.
- Resolve identically titled works by different authors before borrowing dates,
  identifiers or credits from search results.

## Source boundaries

UNESCO's [interview with consultant Devonne Goad](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/confronting-gender-gap-environmental-education)
(9 June 2021; updated 29 April 2026) distinguishes institutional reports she
wrote from a regional analysis she contributed to. It illustrates professional
relevance of applied reports and accurate role labels. It does not disclose her
application entries or prove which works caused her selection.

UNESCO's [Open Science Recommendation](https://www.unesco.org/en/legal-affairs/recommendation-open-science)
(2021) discusses articles, books, research reports and conference papers as
scientific publications, and addresses data and other outputs separately.
This is contextual terminology, not a recruitment-field rule or a requirement
that applicant publications be open access.

The bundled schema establishes observed controls/client configuration. It does
not establish an official publication-type whitelist, preferred count, journal
ranking, authorship policy or hiring success rate. Keep editorial judgments
labelled as recommendations.
