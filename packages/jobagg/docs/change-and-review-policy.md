# JobAgg change and review policy

Applies to fetching, parsing, scheduling, recovery, storage, publication and their reviews. This is the canonical policy; agent instructions link here rather than duplicate it. Explicit task authorization controls the permitted work. This document does not authorize a deployment, a network probe, a hold reset or a schedule change.

Evidence was inspected on **1 October 2026** on `master`, HEAD `f717b5e845257eb745a8aec71c5ca4db812ce531`, with substantial existing local changes. HEAD alone does not identify the deployed implementation. Record the actual branch, HEAD, relevant working-tree delta and deployed fingerprint before a change. The [evidence and enforcement map](change-and-review-evidence.md) distinguishes observed behavior, requirements and gaps. [E0](change-and-review-evidence.md#e0-authority-and-provenance), [E6](change-and-review-evidence.md#e6-changes-validation-and-integrity)

## R1. Classify the demonstrated failure scope

Separate **vacancy-specific unavailability**, **listing-route failure**, and **source/host access failure**. A missing-page body, Workday S22, or one failed detail request alone must not establish organization-wide failure or closure. Bind classification to the exact source, vacancy, request/response URL, phase, status and captured body. A protective host stop is an admission decision, not proof that every vacancy or route is unavailable. Keep unknown denials/challenges subject to review; do not turn every 403 into a missing vacancy. A change of transport must retain the reviewed source and phase boundaries. [E1](change-and-review-evidence.md#e1-failure-scope)

## R2. Make recovery explicit and bounded

Every temporary hold must identify its scope, evidence, next eligibility conditions, cooldown, retry/probe ceiling and outcome at exhaustion. Check the **task and host/source gates together**: an elapsed host cooldown cannot recover a permanently blocked task. Preserve charged attempts, Retry-After, pacing, quota history and probe ownership across restarts. Use the existing applicable limits; changing them requires R6. A bound is a ceiling, not permission to issue a probe. [E2](change-and-review-evidence.md#e2-recovery)

Persistent security, identity, integrity, certificate and ambiguous-content holds require review and must report why there is no automatic retry. Release only an evidenced matching hold after a reviewed correction or explicitly authorized bounded diagnostic; retain unrelated holds and prior evidence. A reviewed recovery requiring a fresh complete listing must enforce that condition at both selection and durable claim. Do not erase controller/policy history, refresh timestamps to imply success, or bulk-reset blocked/dead-letter tasks. The deployed implementation and locally prepared integration differ; see G1 for the captured-response recovery correction and its activation status. [E2](change-and-review-evidence.md#e2-recovery), [E6](change-and-review-evidence.md#e6-changes-validation-and-integrity)

## R3. Preserve enumeration and main-text verification

Preserve the configured board/filter/locale scope, all required pages/cursors, unique stable identities, advertised-count reconciliation and verified-empty evidence. A latest-jobs subset, successful request, parser success, row count or recent observation is not a complete census. Only a newer independently complete inventory can support absence reconciliation; retain the distinction between absent, expired, unavailable and closed. Collector and independent verifier must agree on legitimate terminal pages while rejecting duplicates, truncation, mismatched scope and changing totals. [E3](change-and-review-evidence.md#e3-completeness)

Main job text remains required, including a primary posting/description document where the provider uses one. Preserve exact source/body/identity provenance and verify persistence/publication separately. A stored hash match proves survival of the fetched text, not that all public sections were fetched. Missing independent main-text or metadata coverage must remain explicit; do not report global completeness from successful execution or publication. [E3](change-and-review-evidence.md#e3-completeness), [E4](change-and-review-evidence.md#e4-preservation-and-document-scope)

## R4. Preserve useful data and recover safely

Preserve historical archives, useful prior descriptions, first-seen identity and stored documents/blobs/associations. Listing-only refreshes and failed details must not replace retained full text with teasers or blanks. Shorter replacement text needs the existing source-bound replacement proof or reviewed exception. Supplementary attachment fetching stays disabled under the current user requirement; that exclusion does not waive a primary description document or certify attachments complete. Do not infer current presence from retained historical rows. [E4](change-and-review-evidence.md#e4-preservation-and-document-scope)

For live mutations, use the actual shared owner lock, applicable maintenance/publication gates, a preview tied to current evidence, exact before/after checks and a durable journal. Cleanup may retire only verified eligible disposable artifacts under the retention policy; retain sole copies of fetched data and unresolved recovery evidence. Use the existing sealed publication projection rather than adding an unconditional full-database copy to every tick. Roll back uncommitted transactions; inspect and resume the journal after an uncertain commit. A code rollback must restore a coherent reviewed code/configuration/fingerprint set without overwriting legitimate intervening live data. [E4](change-and-review-evidence.md#e4-preservation-and-document-scope), [E6](change-and-review-evidence.md#e6-changes-validation-and-integrity)

## R5. Preserve budgets, resumability and transport checks

Account for admission, request/body reads, redirects, pagination, independent verification, database work, reporting and publication within the applicable deadlines. Preserve report reserve, source admission floors and separate stage budgets. A budget deferral must remain resumable without being counted as a provider failure or a completed partial inventory. If an independent verifier lacks an inner deadline, state that enforcement gap rather than treating the outer timeout as proof of bounded verification. Preserve completed work and journal-based publication recovery on interruption. [E5](change-and-review-evidence.md#e5-budgets-and-transport)

Keep HTTPS certificate/hostname validation, fresh DNS/IP checks and exact redirect allowlists active in HTTP, browser and diagnostic paths. Distinguish typed unexpected TLS EOF and supported network failures from verified certificate errors; generic SSL errors remain review cases. Never disable certificate validation to recover fetching. A new allowed host needs an evidenced source contract and review; an unverified blocking hypothesis does not authorize broader allowlists, imported browser credentials or IP rotation. A source-specific successful browser comparison does not justify a general transport workaround. [E1](change-and-review-evidence.md#e1-failure-scope), [E5](change-and-review-evidence.md#e5-budgets-and-transport)

## R6. Make behavior changes reviewable and reversible

For concurrency, rate, retry, parser, transport or failure-classification changes, state the affected contracts and expected queue/publication effects. Preserve unrelated limits and state. Include the incident reproduction, relevant successful historical fixture, negative cases, and focused regression checks across the affected collector → capture → verifier → database/publication boundary. A test result applies to the tested scope; record skips, expected failures and live-validation limits. [E6](change-and-review-evidence.md#e6-changes-validation-and-integrity)

Before an authorized activation, specify a bounded validation plan: exact source/task scope, request/time limits, preserved guards, expected evidence and a reviewed rollback/recovery path. Stop the affected validation/recovery path on wrong identity/scope, text or archive loss, TLS/URL-policy weakening, a new access challenge, unexpected state mutation, failed integrity binding, or an unresolved publication transaction; retain evidence and follow the reviewed hold/rollback plan. Use measured conditions and existing limits; do not invent an error percentage or success sample size. Automatic concurrency must use observed eligible work, pressure and verified publication, retaining its controller history. [E5](change-and-review-evidence.md#e5-budgets-and-transport), [E6](change-and-review-evidence.md#e6-changes-validation-and-integrity)

Keep one fingerprint over all package Python modules. Legitimate reviewed changes require an explicit matching reseal; do not exclude maintenance modules or automatically bless arbitrary modifications. In a dirty checkout, preserve unrelated work and identify the exact change set instead of resetting or staging everything. [E0](change-and-review-evidence.md#e0-authority-and-provenance), [E6](change-and-review-evidence.md#e6-changes-validation-and-integrity)

## R7. Review only actionable defects in scope

Report only issues introduced, newly exposed or worsened by the change; unmet explicit requirements; or broken behavior, compatibility or contracts. Exclude optional improvements, general refactors and unrelated pre-existing issues. Every P2-or-higher finding must give concrete triggering conditions, supporting code/test/incident evidence, impact, and why this change must address it. Do not repeat an accepted or rejected finding without new grounds or changed assumptions. Separate a policy enforcement gap from a defect attributable to the reviewed change. [E7](change-and-review-evidence.md#e7-review-scope)

## R8. Report observable state with clear denominators

Give an observation timestamp and timezone. Separate enabled/disabled sources; fresh listings/independently complete inventories; current listed vacancies/retained history; pending/eligible/held/blocked/dead-letter tasks; main text/supplementary attachments; worker acceptance/publication/readback. State the fingerprint and what was actually changed, tested, activated or only proposed. Do not sum overlapping test groups. Explain persistent holds and publication lag with their effective gate or queue position, not an assumed retry time. A normal 15-minute invocation is one bounded batch, not a guarantee of complete coverage every 15 minutes. [E8](change-and-review-evidence.md#e8-reporting)

## Decisions and enforcement

The rules above are requirements for changes and reviews, not a claim that all current paths already enforce them. Consult [coverage, gaps and unresolved decisions](change-and-review-evidence.md#enforcement-gaps-and-decisions) before claiming compliance. No rule here requires publishing private databases, HARs, credentials or raw incident captures into Git; provide a sanitized reproduction or explicit evidence-access limitation for reviewers.
