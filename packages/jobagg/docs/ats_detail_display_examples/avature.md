Role Purpose Under the supervision of Senior Programme Manager, the WASH Engineer will operate under the direct supervision of the Senior Programme Manager or his/her assignees, providing technical expertise to support the design, implementation, supervision, and documentation of Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH) projects.

## Responsibilities
include ensuring high-quality design documentation, transparency, integrity, and compliance with technical requirements, specifications, timelines, and budget constraints. The retainer will utilise specialised engineering software and applications specific to WASH systems. Functions / Key Results Expected
1. Design and Documentation Support: Participate in preparing needs assessments for WASH infrastructure, including water pumping stations, storage facilities, transmission and distribution networks, and produce detailed reports. Participate in preparing/reviewing designs for water supply systems, wastewater management, drainage networks, sanitation facilities, and water metering systems (including district water metering). Support in developing/reviewing Terms of Reference, drawings, technical specifications, scopes of work, bills of quantities, cost estimations, and design calculations for WASH projects. Assist in preparing tender documents for consultancy, supply, and construction works and contribute to proposal evaluations.
2. Works Execution Coordination Support: Collaborate with assigned project team members to support project planning, scheduling, and integrated design development for WASH interventions, including water pumping and transmission networks. Conduct site inspection visits and support in preparing detailed site visit reports, noting progress, issues, and photographic documentation (before, during, and after), along with lessons learned. Support in overseeing contract deliverables, ensuring they align with contract terms, high international standards, and organisational requirements. Participate in coordinating system testing, commissioning, training, and final handover to beneficiaries, with a focus on water pumping, storage, and metering systems.
3. Monitoring and Progress Controls: Support in ensuring contractors’ compliance with Quality Management Plan and Health, Safety, Social, and Environmental (HSSE) plans specific to WASH projects. Participate in coordinating and documenting meetings with contractors, technical supervisors, and other stakeholders, contributing to meeting minutes for WASH-related discussions, including issues related to water transmission, distribution, and metering systems.
4. Technical Review and Quality Assurance: Support in preparing/reviewing comprehensive design documentation, including design briefs, reports, calculations, drawings, specifications, and schedules in accordance with beneficiary requirements and international standards. Assist in reviewing the technical accuracy and suitability of designs for water pumping stations, storage tanks, transmission and distribution networks, and metering systems, offering practical solutions where applicable. Support in ensuring all tasks meet applicable Quality Management Plan (QMP) and Quality Control (QC) standards. Produce/review high-quality AutoCAD drawings and maintain accurate site records for all memos, instructions, and materials related to WASH projects.
5. Knowledge Building and Sharing Support the organization, facilitation and delivery of training and learning sessions on project management. Participate in knowledge-sharing initiatives and Communities of Practice (CoPs), contributing insights and lessons learned. Conduct research on WASH and project management-related topics and contribute to the drafting of reports, summaries and briefing notes Skills Design Engineering, Documentation, Engineering, Quality Assurance
