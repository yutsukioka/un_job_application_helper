In this context, IOEO is establishing a roster of evaluation consultants and inviting applications from qualified mid-level and senior evaluation professionals. Candidates will be assessed through a competitive process based on qualifications, experience and competencies. Successful applicants will be included in the roster and considered for assignments as needs arise, subject to suitability and availability. Inclusion in the roster does not constitute an offer of employment or guarantee for future contractual engagement.
