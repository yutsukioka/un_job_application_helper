### Summary
icddr,b is a Bangladesh-based international health research institute that strives to solve key public health problems through high-quality scientific research and innovation. icddr,b, as a values-based organization, encourages women and people with disabilities. Project objective at a glance The National Nipah Surveillance under the Infectious Diseases

### Division
of icddr,b aims to strengthen hospital-based surveillance, early detection, laboratory confirmation, outbreak investigation, and public health response for Nipah virus infection in Bangladesh. The project supports timely identification of suspected Nipah cases, collection and transportation of biological samples, maintenance of biosafety and infection prevention practices, and generation of high-quality surveillance data to support national preparedness and response. ### Duties and Responsibilities
Collect biological samples from suspected Nipah cases, enrolled patients, close contacts, same-source exposed individuals, and other study participants as required by the surveillance protocol. Maintain appropriate biosafety and infection prevention measures before, during, and after sample collection and processing. Process biological specimens, including aliquoting, serum/plasma separation, sample decontamination, packaging, and preparation for laboratory testing. Maintain cold chain during sample collection, temporary storage, transportation, and transfer to designated laboratories. Store samples according to approved SOPs, study protocols, and laboratory quality management guidelines. Prepare logistics and sample collection materials for hospital surveillance teams, field teams, and outbreak investigation teams. Maintain inventory of laboratory supplies, consumables, PPE, reagents, sample collection materials, and transport logistics. Support laboratory testing-related activities as per approved protocol and supervisor’s instruction. Prepare reagents, disinfectants, and laboratory materials in accordance with approved SOPs. Operate and maintain relevant laboratory equipment, including centrifuge, biosafety cabinet, refrigerator, freezer, micropipette, sample transport box, and other surveillance-related equipment. Ensure regular cleaning, disinfection, calibration support, and maintenance documentation of laboratory equipment. Ensure compliance with institutional guidelines, Good Clinical Laboratory Practice, biosafety procedures, infection prevention and control practices, and study-specific SOPs.
